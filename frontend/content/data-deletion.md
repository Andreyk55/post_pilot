# Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: July 4, 2026

You can request deletion of your Publish Harbor account and associated app data.

To request deletion, email support@publishharbor.com from the email address connected to your Publish Harbor account so we can verify the request.

If you want to remove Publish Harbor's access to Meta, you can also disconnect Meta from the Connected Accounts page in the app.

Depending on your request and account state, the data we may delete or anonymize includes:

- Account data
- Workspace data
- Connected Meta account data
- Facebook Page and Instagram account metadata
- Scheduled posts
- Publishing history
- Uploaded media
- Related logs

Please note that some data may be retained where required for security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or operational reasons.

Deleting your data may prevent Publish Harbor from publishing scheduled posts and may remove your ability to view publishing history.

If you have questions about your request, include them in your email and we will review them with the deletion request.
