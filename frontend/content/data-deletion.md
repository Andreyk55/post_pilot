# Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: July 4, 2026

You can delete your Publish Harbor account and associated app data from within the app.

To delete your account:

- Sign in to Publish Harbor.
- Go to Settings.
- Open Account Settings.
- In the Danger Zone, choose Delete Publish Harbor account.
- Follow the confirmation instructions.

Deleting your Publish Harbor account removes associated Publish Harbor app data, including:

- Account data
- Workspace data
- Provider connections
- Connected Meta account data
- Facebook Page and Instagram account metadata
- Scheduled posts
- Drafts
- Uploaded media
- Bucket files
- Publishing history
- Related app records and logs where applicable

Deleting your Publish Harbor account does not delete posts already published to Facebook or Instagram. To remove published posts, delete them directly in Facebook or Instagram.

Please note that some data may be retained where required for security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or operational reasons.

Deleting your data may prevent Publish Harbor from publishing scheduled posts and may remove your ability to view publishing history.
